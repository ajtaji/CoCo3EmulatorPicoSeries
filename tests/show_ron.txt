# Leave Disk Extended Color BASIC on the Pico W's panel with a short program and its output on screen.
expect 15 CoCo 3 ROM loaded
screen 20 \r\nOK +\r\n
line 10 PRINT "COCO 3 ON A PICO W"
line 20 FOR I=1 TO 6:PRINT I;I*I;I*I*I:NEXT
line 30 PRINT "PUREMETAL - 6809 CORE"
wait 2
line CLS:LIST
screen 10 30 PRINT
line RUN
screen 15 PUREMETAL - 6809 CORE +\r\nOK
capture build/show_ron.png
